## Overview

The Seedkeeper App generates passwords directly on your device and stores them there - nothing is sent anywhere else. You control the length and style of each password you create.

If you're unsure what to pick, length matters more than complexity rules, and a machine-generated password beats anything you would invent - see [What is a Strong Password?](/docs/security-academy/password-security/what-is-a-strong-password).

## Step-by-Step Instructions

<div class="step-layout">

<div class="step-text">

### Step 1: Launch the app and scan your Seedkeeper

Open the Seedkeeper App and scan your card or ring, then enter your personal PIN.<br />
See the [First Setup Guide](/docs/product-documentation/getting-started/first-setup-guide) if you haven't set up your PIN yet.

### Step 2: Start generating a secret

Tap **Generate a Secret**, then **Password**.

### Step 3: Label your password

Specify a **label** - this is what distinguishes this secret from all the others in your list.

### Step 4: Add optional details

- **Login** (optional) - an email address, username, or nickname
- **URL** (optional) - the site this password is for

### Step 5: Choose length and style

Pick the password length and style that suits you:
- **Upper case, lower case, symbols** - a fully random string from the character sets you enable
- **Easy-to-read** - a word-based password built from English dictionary words, easier to remember

### Step 6: Generate

Tap **Generate** to see your password.<br />
You can regenerate as many times as you like until you get one you're happy with.

### Step 7: Save it to your device

Tap **Import**, then scan your card or ring again to store the password.

Your new password is now saved on your Seedkeeper.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary>Are the login and URL fields required?</summary>

No, both are optional. See [Add optional details](#step-4-add-optional-details) above.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I change the password after generating it?</summary>

Yes - regenerate as many times as you like before tapping Import. See [Generate](#step-6-generate) above.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where is my password stored?</summary>

Directly on your Seedkeeper device within the EAL6+ certified secure element - there's no cloud involved.<br />
See [Introduction](/docs/product-documentation/getting-started/intro) for more on how Seedkeeper works.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What's the difference between "Easy to read" and randomized passwords?</summary>

"Easy to read" passwords are dictionary-based - built from English words, so they're both easy to read and easy to remember.<br />
Randomized passwords use a charset you specify (upper case, lower case, symbols) instead, which is less memorable but not tied to real words.<br />
See [Choose length and style](#step-5-choose-length-and-style) above.

</details>

## Overview

Deleting removes a secret from your Seedkeeper's secure element for good - there's no undo, and no cloud copy to restore from. If you're deleting a password in order to update it, copy its details first - see [Editing a Password](/docs/product-documentation/password-manager/editing-a-password).

## Step-by-Step Instructions

<div class="step-layout">

<div class="step-text">

### Step 1: Select the secret

Open the entry you want to remove from your secrets list.

### Step 2: Tap Delete

You can optionally reveal the secret to be sure you are deleting the right one. 
If you are sure this is the right secret to delete, tap the "Delete" button.

### Step 3: Confirm

Tick the checkbox to confirm - this operation is irreversible.

### Step 4: Scan your card

Deletion is complete once the scan finishes.

</div>

## FAQ

<details>
<summary>Can I undo a deletion?</summary>

No. Deletion is permanent - there's no cloud copy or undo. If you have a [Backup device](/docs/product-documentation/getting-started/backup-and-recovery), the deleted entry may still exist there if the backup was made before the deletion.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I want to update a password - should I delete it first?</summary>

Copy its current details first, then delete it, then recreate it with your changes. See [Editing a Password](/docs/product-documentation/password-manager/editing-a-password) for the full explanation.

</details>

On a Seedkeeper, backup is a device-to-device operation: a Master device and a Backup device are scanned together, and the Backup ends up holding an encrypted copy of the password manager. Passkeys and OTP credentials are not copied this way. See [Backup & Recovery](/docs/product-documentation/getting-started/backup-and-recovery).

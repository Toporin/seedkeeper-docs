The Client to Authenticator Protocol governs communication between your browser and your authenticator. Together with WebAuthn, which covers the website-to-browser side, it forms FIDO2. Seedkeeper PRO implements CTAP2.1. See [What is FIDO2?](/docs/security-academy/passkeys/what-is-fido2)

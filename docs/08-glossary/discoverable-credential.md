A discoverable credential holds the account reference on the device itself, so the service learns who you are from the credential. Non-discoverable credentials store nothing on the device and require you to identify yourself first. Seedkeeper PRO holds up to 90 discoverable credentials. See [How Passkeys Work](/docs/security-academy/passkeys/how-passkeys-work).

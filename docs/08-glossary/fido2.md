FIDO2 is a **specification**, not a product or a company: a public document describing how authentication should work, which anyone is free to implement. It is published jointly by the **FIDO Alliance**, an industry consortium, and the **W3C**, the body that standardises the web.

It covers the whole path from website to hardware, in two halves:

- **[WebAuthn](/docs/glossary/webauthn)** handles the conversation between the website and the browser, including the domain check that makes phishing impossible.
- **[CTAP2](/docs/glossary/ctap2)** handles the conversation between the browser and your authenticator, over NFC, USB or Bluetooth.

Because it is an open standard with no licence fee and no gatekeeper, a certified device works with any compliant service without either party knowing the other exists. That is what a [passkey](/docs/glossary/passkey) is built on, and why changing browser, phone or operating system does not strip you of your credentials.

Seedkeeper PRO is **FIDO2 Level 1 certified**, certificate `FA001202600016`. Learn more in [What is FIDO2?](/docs/security-academy/passkeys/what-is-fido2)

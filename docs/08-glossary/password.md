A password is a secret string of characters - letters, numbers, symbols, or a mix - shared between you and a service and used to prove your identity when signing in. Because both sides hold it, either side can leak it, which is the structural weakness passkeys were designed to remove.

Learn more in [What is a Password?](/docs/security-academy/password-security/what-is-a-password)
